Israel Travel Guide

Explore the Holy Land

Western Wall

A historic landmark in Jerusalem's Old City.

Dead Sea

A unique desert lake famous for floating and mineral-rich water.

Tel Aviv Beach

A Mediterranean beach area with city life, food, and nightlife.

Recommendations

Visit the Western Wall at sunrise or sunset for a unique experience. Float in the Dead Sea and enjoy the scenic views. Relax on Tel Aviv Beach and explore the city's vibrant nightlife.
