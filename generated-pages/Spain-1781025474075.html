Spain Travel Guide

Discover the beauty and culture of Spain, from its stunning beaches to its vibrant cities.

Alhambra: A historic palace and fortress complex in Granada, showcasing Moorish architecture.

Park Güell: A public park in Barcelona, featuring stunning architecture and city views.

Museo del Prado: A world-renowned art museum in Madrid, housing an extensive collection of European art.

Recommendations:

  • Try the local cuisine, including tapas and paella.
  • Visit the famous beaches of the Costa Brava and Costa del Sol.
  • Explore the historic cities of Madrid, Barcelona, and Seville.
