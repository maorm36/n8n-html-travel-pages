France Travel Guide

Top Attractions

Eiffel Tower

The iconic symbol of Paris and one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.

Louvre Museum

One of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts.

Mont Saint-Michel

A picturesque island town surrounded by vast bay and towering walls, a must-visit for any traveler.

Recommendations

Try the famous French cuisine, including escargots, croissants, and cheese. Don't forget to visit the charming villages and towns, such as Annecy and Colmar.
