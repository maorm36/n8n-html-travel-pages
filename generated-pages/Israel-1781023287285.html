Israel Travel Guide

Must-see Attractions

Masada

An ancient fortress situated atop a rock plateau, overlooking the Dead Sea.

Western Wall

A sacred site in Jerusalem, considered the holiest place in Judaism.

Caesarea

An ancient Roman city, featuring impressive ruins and a beautiful beach.

Recommendations

Try the local cuisine, including falafel, shawarma, and hummus. Visit the vibrant cities of Tel Aviv and Jerusalem, and explore the stunning natural beauty of the Galilee and the Negev.
